# AI Lawyer

AI Lawyer is an AI-powered legal assistant designed to help users understand legal concepts, generate legal documents, and provide insights based on legal data.

## Features

- **Legal Document Generation** – Draft contracts, agreements, and legal letters.
- **Legal Research** – Get insights into legal precedents and case laws.
- **AI-Powered Assistance** – Ask legal questions and receive AI-driven responses.
